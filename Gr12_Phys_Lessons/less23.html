📋 Topic Summary

🔑 Key Energy Conversions

Generators: Mechanical Energy → Electrical Energy
Motors: Electrical Energy → Mechanical Energy
🔄

AC Generator

  • ✓ Uses slip rings
  • ✓ Produces alternating current
  • ✓ Current changes direction every half turn
  • ✓ Used in power stations
  • ✓ Voltage can be stepped up/down
⚡

DC Generator

  • ✓ Uses split-ring commutator
  • ✓ Produces direct current
  • ✓ Current flows in one direction
  • ✓ Used for electroplating
  • ✓ Charges batteries in vehicles
⚙️

DC Motor

  • ✓ Uses split-ring commutator
  • ✓ Force on current-carrying coil creates torque
  • ✓ Commutator reverses current for continuous rotation
  • ✓ Brushes maintain electrical contact
  • ✓ Momentum carries coil past dead point
💡 Key Relationship: Both motors and generators involve a coil rotating in a magnetic field. The main difference is the direction of energy conversion!

📊 Comparison Table

✅ Ways to Improve Output/Efficiency

For Generators:

  • Use stronger magnets
  • Increase number of coils/turns
  • Spin the coil faster

For Motors:

  • Increase strength of permanent magnet
  • Increase induced EMF
  • Increase number of turns in the coil

🔌 AC vs DC Conversion

To convert AC generator to DC generator:

Replace the slip rings with a split-ring commutator. This ensures current in the external circuit does not change direction.


AC motor vs DC motor:

An AC motor works without a commutator since the current already alternates. A DC motor needs a commutator to reverse current direction.
