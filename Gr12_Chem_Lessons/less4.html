Key Principle

Σpbefore = Σpafter
m1v1i + m2v2i = m1v1f + m2v2f

Where:

  • m = mass (kg)
  • vi = initial velocity (m/s)
  • vf = final velocity (m/s)

Main Body

+

Principle of Conservation of Momentum

The principle of conservation of momentum states: the total linear momentum in an isolated system remains constant in both magnitude and direction.

In other words, in an isolated system:

Total momentum before collision = Total momentum after collision

An isolated system means no external forces are acting on the system.

In simple terms, if one object loses momentum, the other gains exactly that amount of momentum.

Worked Examples

Problem 1: Masses Moving in Opposite Directions

A 9 kg mass travelling eastwards at 4 m/s collides with a 3 kg mass travelling westward at 8 m/s. After collision the 9 kg mass is moving westward at 1 m/s. Calculate the velocity of the 3 kg mass after collision.

Interactive Calculator for Problem 1

Set up the conservation of momentum equation

Take east as positive:

Σpbefore = Σpafter

m1v1i + m2v2i = m1v1f + m2v2f

(9 × 4) + (3 × -8) = (9 × -1) + (3 × v2f)

Solve for the unknown velocity

36 + (-24) = -9 + 3v2f

12 = -9 + 3v2f

21 = 3v2f

v2f = 7 m/s

Since the value is positive, the 3 kg mass moves eastward at 7 m/s after the collision.

Problem 2: Railway Trucks Collision

A railway truck with a mass of 5000 kg and a velocity of 6 m/s collides with a second truck. The second truck has a mass of 4000 kg, and before the collision, it had a velocity of 3 m/s in the same direction as the first truck. The two trucks separate after the collision. The new velocity of the second truck is 5 m/s in the same direction as before. Calculate the new velocity of the first truck.

Interactive Calculator for Problem 2

Set up the conservation of momentum equation

Let the direction of the initial velocity be positive:

Σpbefore = Σpafter

m1v1i + m2v2i = m1v1f + m2v2f

(5000 × 6) + (4000 × 3) = (5000 × v1f) + (4000 × 5)

Solve for the unknown velocity

30000 + 12000 = 5000v1f + 20000

42000 = 5000v1f + 20000

22000 = 5000v1f

v1f = 4.4 m/s

The first truck moves at 4.4 m/s in the same direction as its initial velocity.

Problem 3: Toy Cars Collision

Two toy cars are approaching each other. Car 1, with mass 0.8 kg, has a velocity of 5 m/s to the right. Car 2, has a mass of 1 kg and a velocity of 5 m/s to the left. After the collision, car 2 is stationary. Calculate car 1's velocity after the collision.

Interactive Calculator for Problem 3

Set up the conservation of momentum equation

Take right as positive:

Σpbefore = Σpafter

m1v1i + m2v2i = m1v1f + m2v2f

(0.8 × 5) + (1 × -5) = (0.8 × v1f) + (1 × 0)

Solve for the unknown velocity

4 + (-5) = 0.8v1f + 0

-1 = 0.8v1f

v1f = -1.25 m/s

Since the value is negative, car 1 moves to the left at 1.25 m/s after the collision.
