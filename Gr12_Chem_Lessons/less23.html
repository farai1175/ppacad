Overview

Main Content

1. The Auto-ionisation of Water

Water undergoes auto-ionisation, in which a proton (H+) is transferred from one water molecule to another. This process creates hydronium ions (H3O+) and hydroxide ions (OH−).

H2O(l) + H2O(l) ⇌ H3O+(aq) + OH−(aq)

Ionic Product of Water (Kw)

Kw = [H3O+][OH−] = 1 × 10−14

(at 25°C / room temperature)

📌 Key Points:

  • ✦ In a neutral solution: [H3O+] = [OH−] = 1 × 10−7 mol·dm−3
  • ✦ The ionic product Kw remains constant at 1 × 10−14 in any aqueous solution
  • ✦ This relationship helps us calculate pH of bases

2. Calculating pH of Strong Bases

For strong bases like NaOH and KOH, we can calculate pH using the relationship between [OH−] and Kw.

pH Calculation Formula

[H3O+] = Kw / [OH−] = 10−14 / [OH−]

pH = −log[H3O+]

📝 Worked Example 1

Question: Calculate the pH of a NaOH solution with concentration 1 × 10−4 mol·dm−3

Step 1: Write the dissociation equation
NaOH(s) → Na+(aq) + OH−(aq)
1 molecule of NaOH produces 1 OH− ion
Step 2: Find [OH−]
[NaOH] = [OH−] = 1 × 10−4 mol·dm−3
Step 3: Calculate [H3O+]
[H3O+] = Kw / [OH−] = (1 × 10−14) / (1 × 10−4) = 1 × 10−10 mol·dm−3
Step 4: Calculate pH
pH = −log(1 × 10−10) = 10
📝 Worked Example 2

Question: Calculate the pH of a NaOH solution with concentration 1 × 10−2 M

Solution:
[OH−] = 1 × 10−2 mol·dm−3
[H3O+] = (1 × 10−14) / (1 × 10−2) = 1 × 10−12 mol·dm−3
pH = −log(1 × 10−12) = 12
📝 Worked Example 3

Question: 1.2 g KOH is dissolved in water and made up to 425 cm³. Calculate the pH.

Step 1: Calculate moles of KOH (Mr = 39 + 16 + 1 = 56 g/mol)
n = m/M = 1.2/56 = 0.0214 mol
Step 2: Calculate concentration
V = 425 cm³ = 0.425 dm³
[KOH] = n/V = 0.0214/0.425 = 0.0504 mol·dm−3
Step 3: Calculate [H3O+] and pH
[OH−] = 0.0504 mol·dm−3
[H3O+] = (1 × 10−14) / 0.0504 = 1.98 × 10−13
pH = −log(1.98 × 10−13) ≈ 12.7

3. Salt Hydrolysis

A salt is formed when an acid reacts with a base. Hydrolysis is the ability of ions from the salt to react with water molecules, thus altering the pH of the solution.

📌 Rules for Predicting Salt pH:

  • 🔵 Strong acid + Strong base → pH = 7 (neutral, no hydrolysis)
  • 🔴 Strong acid + Weak base → pH < 7 (acidic, hydrolysis occurs)
  • 🟢 Weak acid + Strong base → pH > 7 (basic, hydrolysis occurs)
Salt Acid Base Result pH
NaCl HCl (strong) NaOH (strong) Neutral = 7
NH₄Cl HCl (strong) NH₃ (weak) Acidic < 7
CH₃COONa CH₃COOH (weak) NaOH (strong) Basic > 7
KNO₃ HNO₃ (strong) KOH (strong) Neutral = 7
CuSO₄ H₂SO₄ (strong) Cu(OH)₂ (weak) Acidic < 7
