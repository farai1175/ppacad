Lesson Overview

Organic chemistry is the study of carbon-containing compounds. In this lesson, we'll explore hydrocarbons – compounds made up of only carbon and hydrogen atoms. Understanding hydrocarbons is fundamental to organic chemistry as they form the backbone of more complex organic molecules.

💡

Why is Carbon Special?

Carbon has a valency of 4, meaning it can form four covalent bonds. This, combined with its ability to form long chains (called catenation), makes carbon incredibly versatile in forming millions of different compounds!

🔄

Pre-Knowledge Review

Before we dive into hydrocarbons, let's review some essential concepts:

🔗 Covalent Bonds

Formed by the sharing of electrons between two atoms. Generally occurs between two non-metals.

🧲 Intermolecular Forces

Forces that exist between molecules and hold them together. These affect physical properties like boiling point.

⚛️ Carbon's Valency

Carbon has a valency of 4 and 4 valence electrons. It can form 4 covalent bonds.

⛓️ Catenation

The ability of carbon to form long chains by bonding with other carbon atoms.

⚗️

What are Hydrocarbons?

📚

Definition

Hydrocarbons are organic compounds that contain only carbon (C) and hydrogen (H) atoms. They are the simplest type of organic compounds.

⛓️

Aliphatic Hydrocarbons

Aliphatic hydrocarbons are classified based on the type of bonds between carbon atoms:

ALKANES Saturated
C — C

Only single bonds between carbon atoms

General Formula: CnH2n+2

ALKENES Unsaturated
C = C

At least one double bond between carbon atoms

General Formula: CnH2n

ALKYNES Unsaturated
C ≡ C

At least one triple bond between carbon atoms

General Formula: CnH2n-2

🔬

Saturated vs Unsaturated

Saturated hydrocarbons (alkanes) have only single bonds – all carbon atoms are "saturated" with hydrogen atoms.

Unsaturated hydrocarbons (alkenes and alkynes) have double or triple bonds – they can accept more hydrogen atoms.

🧪

Testing for Saturated and Unsaturated Compounds

🧫 Bromine Water Test

  1. Add a few drops of bromine water (orange-brown) to the compound
  2. Shake the solution
  3. Observe the colour change
🟠 → ⚪
Colour disappears immediately

= UNSATURATED (alkene or alkyne)

🟠 → 🟠
Colour remains

= SATURATED (alkane)

📝

Representing Hydrocarbons

Hydrocarbons can be represented in three different ways:

1️⃣ Molecular Formula

Shows only the number and type of atoms in the molecule.

C₂H₆
2️⃣ Condensed Structural Formula

Shows how atoms are grouped together, but not all bonds.

CH₃CH₃
3️⃣ Structural Formula

Shows all atoms and all bonds in the molecule.

    H   H
    |   |
H — C — C — H
    |   |
    H   H
📊

A homologous series is a family of compounds with the same functional group and similar chemical properties. Each member differs by CH₂.
