Lesson Overview

+

This lesson focuses on applying the conservation of momentum principle to specific scenarios: collisions where objects stick together (perfectly inelastic collisions) and explosions. In both cases, the total momentum of the system is conserved.

Key Formulas

For objects sticking together: m₁v₁ᵢ + m₂v₂ᵢ = (m₁ + m₂)vf
For explosions: 0 = m₁v₁f + m₂v₂f

Where momentum before explosion is zero.

Collisions Where Objects Stick Together

In perfectly inelastic collisions, objects stick together after collision and move with a common velocity. The conservation of momentum equation becomes:

m₁v₁ᵢ + m₂v₂ᵢ = (m₁ + m₂)vf

Explosion Scenarios

In explosion scenarios, objects start together at rest and then move apart. The total momentum before the explosion is zero, so the total momentum after must also be zero.

0 = m₁v₁f + m₂v₂f

This means the objects move in opposite directions with momenta that are equal in magnitude but opposite in direction.

Worked Examples

Problem 1: Bullet and Wooden Block

A 30 g bullet is shot horizontally with a certain velocity into a wooden block of mass 1.47 kg, which is standing on a smooth surface. After the impact the bullet gets embedded in the wood and they move off together with a horizontal velocity of 4 m/s. Calculate the initial velocity of the bullet.

Interactive Calculator for Problem 1

Set up the conservation of momentum equation

Take the initial velocity of the bullet as positive:

Σpbefore = Σpafter

m₁v₁ᵢ + m₂v₂ᵢ = (m₁ + m₂)vf

(0.03 × v₁ᵢ) + (1.47 × 0) = (0.03 + 1.47) × 4

Solve for the unknown velocity

0.03v₁ᵢ + 0 = 1.5 × 4

0.03v₁ᵢ = 6

v₁ᵢ = 200 m/s

The initial velocity of the bullet is 200 m/s.

Problem 2: Car Collision

The driver of a 1800 kg car stops the car when he sees that the robot he is approaching turns red. A smaller car, whose driver tries to 'beat' the red light, collides into the back of the stationary car and the two cars become entangled during the collision. If the smaller car, of mass 900 kg, was moving at 20 m/s when it hit the stationary car, calculate the velocity of the entangled wreckage after the collision.

Interactive Calculator for Problem 2

Set up the conservation of momentum equation

Take initial velocity as positive:

Σpbefore = Σpafter

m₁v₁ᵢ + m₂v₂ᵢ = (m₁ + m₂)vf

(1800 × 0) + (900 × 20) = (1800 + 900) × vf

Solve for the unknown velocity

0 + 18000 = 2700 × vf

18000 = 2700vf

vf = 6.67 m/s

The entangled wreckage moves at 6.67 m/s.

Problem 3: Rifle Recoil

A bullet with a mass of 50 g leaves the barrel of a rifle with a velocity of 400 m/s. If the mass of the rifle is 3 kg, calculate the recoil velocity of the rifle.

Interactive Calculator for Problem 3

Set up the conservation of momentum equation

Take the velocity of the bullet as positive. Initial momentum is zero:

Σpbefore = Σpafter

0 = m₁v₁f + m₂v₂f

0 = (0.05 × 400) + (3 × v₂f)

Solve for the unknown velocity

0 = 20 + 3v₂f

3v₂f = -20

v₂f = -6.67 m/s

The rifle recoils at 6.67 m/s in the opposite direction to the bullet.
