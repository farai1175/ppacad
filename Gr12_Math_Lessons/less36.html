Quick Identity Reference

Review these key identities before starting the assessment:

Compound Sine
sin(A ± B) = sin A cos B ± cos A sin B
Compound Cosine
cos(A ± B) = cos A cos B ∓ sin A sin B
Double Angle Sine
sin 2x = 2 sin x cos x
Double Angle Cosine
cos 2x = cos²x - sin²x = 2cos²x - 1 = 1 - 2sin²x
Pythagorean Identity
sin²x + cos²x = 1
Quotient Identity
tan x = sin x / cos x
