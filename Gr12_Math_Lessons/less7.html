Geometric Sequences and Series

Lesson 2: Solving for Unknown Terms Using Simultaneous Equations

0% Complete

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • 1
    Given two terms of a geometric sequence, solve simultaneously to determine the sequence and apply the general term formula to find another term.
  • 2
    Apply the definition of a geometric sequence to find terms when they are expressed in terms of a variable.
  • 3
    Apply the general term formula in a variety of questions to gain more practice with geometric sequences.

📖 Overview

In this lesson, we will explore advanced techniques for working with geometric sequences. You'll learn how to determine the first term and common ratio when given two terms from a sequence, using simultaneous equations. We'll also work with algebraic expressions as terms of a sequence.

Tn = a · rn−1
General Term Formula for Geometric Sequences

Where:

  • Tn = the nth term of the sequence
  • a = the first term
  • r = the common ratio
  • n = the term position

Key Concepts

📌 Definition: In a geometric sequence, each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term by a constant value called the common ratio (r).

📌 Common Ratio Formula:

r = T2 ÷ T1 = T3 ÷ T2 = Tn+1 ÷ Tn

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1 Finding a term using simultaneous equations

Problem: The third term of a geometric sequence is 9 and the sixth term is 243. Find the tenth term.

Solution:

1

Set up equations using the general term formula:

T3 = a · r2 = 9 ... (1)
T6 = a · r5 = 243 ... (2)
2

Divide equation (2) by equation (1) to eliminate 'a':

(a · r5) ÷ (a · r2) = 243 ÷ 9
r3 = 27
r = 3
3

Substitute r = 3 into equation (1) to find 'a':

a · (3)2 = 9
a · 9 = 9
a = 1
4

Find the tenth term using a = 1, r = 3, n = 10:

T10 = 1 · (3)9 = 19683

✓ The tenth term is 19683

Example 2 Finding x when terms contain variables

Problem: (x+1), (2x+2), (6x−4) are the first three terms of a geometric sequence. Find x if x > 0, and hence find the first three terms.

Solution:

1

Use the property that the ratio between consecutive terms is constant:

T2 ÷ T1 = T3 ÷ T2
(2x+2) ÷ (x+1) = (6x−4) ÷ (2x+2)
2

Cross multiply:

(2x+2)(2x+2) = (6x−4)(x+1)
4x² + 8x + 4 = 6x² + 2x − 4
3

Simplify and solve:

0 = 2x² − 6x − 8
0 = 2(x² − 3x − 4)
0 = 2(x − 4)(x + 1)
x = 4 or x = −1
4

Since x > 0, we have x = 4. Find the terms:

T1 = x + 1 = 4 + 1 = 5
T2 = 2x + 2 = 2(4) + 2 = 10
T3 = 6x − 4 = 6(4) − 4 = 20

✓ The sequence is 5, 10, 20, ... with r = 2
