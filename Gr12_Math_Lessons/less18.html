Graph Applications

Grade: 12
Week: Term 1 Week 4
Time: 30 minutes
Topic: Functions – Graph Applications

Learn to find lengths, coordinates, intersection points, and transformed equations from graphs of functions.

✓ Lesson Objectives

▼

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Find equations of functions, asymptotes, and axes of symmetry
  • Determine lengths between points and coordinates of key features
  • Find points of intersection by solving equations simultaneously
  • Apply transformations (shifts, reflections, stretches) and write new equations
  • Interpret graphical information to solve exam-style problems

📚 Core Concepts

▼

1. Finding Lengths on a Graph

Remember: Length is always positive.

  • Horizontal length = |x₂ – x₁| = larger x – smaller x
  • Vertical length = |y₂ – y₁| = top y – bottom y

2. Coordinates of Points

Any point on a graph is written as (x, y).

To find coordinates:

  • Intercepts: Set x=0 (y-intercept) or y=0 (x-intercept)
  • Turning point: Use vertex formula or derivative
  • Asymptotes: Identify limits of the function

3. Points of Intersection

To find where two graphs meet:

  • Set their equations equal: f(x) = g(x)
  • Solve for x
  • Substitute back to find y

4. Function Transformations

Given y = f(x):
• y = f(x) + k → vertical shift up by k
• y = f(x + p) → horizontal shift left by p
• y = -f(x) → reflection over x-axis
• y = f(-x) → reflection over y-axis
• y = a·f(x) → vertical stretch by |a|
• y = f(bx) → horizontal compression by 1/|b|

💡 Worked Examples

▼

Example 1: Finding Length

Points A(2, 5) and B(7, 1). Find AB (vertical and horizontal components).
  • Horizontal length = 7 – 2 = 5 units
  • Vertical length = 5 – 1 = 4 units
  • Actual distance = √(5² + 4²) = √41 (if needed)

Example 2: Intersection of y = 2x + 1 and y = -x + 4

  • Set equal: 2x + 1 = -x + 4
  • 3x = 3 → x = 1
  • y = 2(1) + 1 = 3
  • Intersection at (1, 3)

Example 3: Transformation

Given f(x) = 1/x. Write equation after: reflection over x-axis, then shift right 2 and up 3.
  • Reflect: y = -1/x
  • Right 2: y = -1/(x – 2)
  • Up 3: y = -1/(x – 2) + 3
  • Final: y = 3 – 1/(x – 2)

❓ Quick Quiz (6 Questions)

▼

Test your understanding of graph applications.

Q1: What is the horizontal length between (3, 2) and (8, 2)?

A) 2
B) 5
C) 0
D) 10
Answer: 8 – 3 = 5

Q2: Where do y = x² and y = 4 intersect?

A) (0, 0)
B) (2, 4)
C) (-2, 4)
D) Both B and C
Answer: x² = 4 → x = ±2

Q3: After reflecting y = 2x over the x-axis, what is the new equation?

A) y = -2x
B) y = 2x
C) y = -x/2
D) y = 2/x
Answer: Reflection → multiply by -1

Q4: Which is always positive?

A) x-coordinate
B) Length
C) Slope
D) y-intercept
Answer: Length is a distance → never negative

Q5: What is the y-intercept of y = 3/(x+1)?

A) 3
B) 0
C) Undefined
D) -1
Answer: Set x=0 → y = 3/(0+1) = 3

Q6: Vertical asymptote of y = 2/(x - 5)?

A) x = 0
B) x = 5
C) y = 5
D) y = 2
Answer: Denominator = 0 → x = 5
