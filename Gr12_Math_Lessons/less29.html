Topic: Compound Angles

Lesson 2 (60 minutes)

Simplifying Expressions Using Compound Angle Identities

Recognizing patterns and using special angles to simplify trigonometric expressions

📚 Learning Objectives

  • Recognize the expanded form of compound angle identities
  • Simplify trigonometric expressions by identifying compound angle patterns
  • Use special angles (30°, 45°, 60°, 90°) to evaluate simplified expressions
  • Distinguish between sine and cosine compound angle patterns
  • Convert expanded expressions back to compact form

📋 Overview

In the previous lesson, we learned the compound angle identities and how to expand expressions like sin(A + B). In this lesson, we'll work in the reverse direction - we'll learn to recognize when an expression matches a compound angle pattern and simplify it back to a single trigonometric function.

🎯 Key Skill

Pattern Recognition: If you see sin A cos B ± cos A sin B, it's sin(A ± B)

If you see cos A cos B ∓ sin A sin B, it's cos(A ∓ B)

📐 Main Concepts & Principles

1. Review: Special Angle Values

These values are essential for evaluating simplified expressions:

Angle 0° 30° 45° 60° 90°
sin θ 0 ½ √2/2 √3/2 1
cos θ 1 √3/2 √2/2 ½ 0
tan θ 0 √3/3 1 √3 undefined

2. Review: Compound Angle Identities

SINE IDENTITIES
sin(A + B) = sin A cos B + cos A sin B sin(A - B) = sin A cos B - cos A sin B

Functions ALTERNATE: sin, cos, cos, sin

COSINE IDENTITIES
cos(A + B) = cos A cos B - sin A sin B cos(A - B) = cos A cos B + sin A sin B

Functions TOGETHER: cos cos, sin sin

3. Recognizing Sine Compound Angle Patterns

🔍 How to Recognize sin(A ± B)

Look for: sin _ cos _ ± cos _ sin _

  • The functions alternate (sin, cos, cos, sin)
  • The sign between terms is the same as in the compound angle
  • The angles appear in the same order in both products

Example 1: Simplify sin 30° cos 60° + cos 30° sin 60°

1 Identify the pattern: sin _ cos _ + cos _ sin _ → This is sin(A + B)

2 Identify A and B: A = 30°, B = 60°

3 Write in compact form: sin(30° + 60°) = sin 90°

4 Evaluate using special angles: sin 90° = 1

Example 2: Simplify sin 60° cos 30° - cos 60° sin 30°

1 Identify the pattern: sin _ cos _ - cos _ sin _ → This is sin(A - B)

2 Identify A and B: A = 60°, B = 30°

3 Write in compact form: sin(60° - 30°) = sin 30°

4 Evaluate: sin 30° = ½

💡 Important: sin 30° cos 60° + cos 30° sin 60° looks different from sin 60° cos 30° + cos 60° sin 30°, but they're EQUAL! Both simplify to sin 90° = 1. Order doesn't matter because A + B = B + A.

4. Recognizing Cosine Compound Angle Patterns

🔍 How to Recognize cos(A ± B)

Look for: cos _ cos _ ∓ sin _ sin _

  • The functions stay together (cos cos, sin sin)
  • The sign between terms is OPPOSITE to the sign in the compound angle!
  • + between products → cos(A - B)
  • - between products → cos(A + B)

Example 3: Simplify cos 70° cos 25° + sin 70° sin 25°

1 Identify the pattern: cos _ cos _ + sin _ sin _ → This is cos(A - B)

(Remember: + between terms means - in the angle)

2 Identify A and B: A = 70°, B = 25°

3 Write in compact form: cos(70° - 25°) = cos 45°

4 Evaluate: cos 45° = √2/2 (or 1/√2)

Example 4: Simplify cos 25° cos 35° - sin 25° sin 35°

1 Identify the pattern: cos _ cos _ - sin _ sin _ → This is cos(A + B)

(Remember: - between terms means + in the angle)

2 Identify A and B: A = 25°, B = 35°

3 Write in compact form: cos(25° + 35°) = cos 60°

4 Evaluate: cos 60° = ½

5. More Examples with Special Angles

Example 5: Simplify sin 25° cos 35° + cos 25° sin 35°

1 Pattern: sin _ cos _ + cos _ sin _ → sin(A + B)

2 = sin(25° + 35°) = sin 60°

3 = √3/2

🔑 Quick Reference: Sign Rules

If you see... It equals... Memory Tip
sin A cos B + cos A sin B sin(A + B) Signs MATCH
sin A cos B − cos A sin B sin(A − B) Signs MATCH
cos A cos B + sin A sin B cos(A − B) Signs OPPOSITE
cos A cos B − sin A sin B cos(A + B) Signs OPPOSITE
