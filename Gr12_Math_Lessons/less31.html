Topic: Compound Angles

Lesson 4 (60 minutes)

Reduction Formulae & Combined Techniques

Expressing angles in other quadrants as acute angles and combining with compound angle identities

📚 Learning Objectives

  • Recall and apply reduction formulae for all four quadrants
  • Express angles greater than 90° as acute angles with appropriate signs
  • Combine reduction formulae with compound angle identities
  • Simplify complex trigonometric expressions
  • Prove sum-to-product identities

📋 Overview

This lesson consolidates your understanding of trigonometry by combining reduction formulae (from Grade 11) with compound angle identities. The key skill is converting angles in any quadrant to their acute angle equivalents with the correct signs, then applying compound angle identities to simplify expressions.

🎯 Key Skill

Any angle can be expressed in terms of an acute angle θ (where θ < 90°) using reduction formulae. The sign depends on which quadrant the original angle is in.

📐 Main Concepts & Principles

1. Reduction Formulae by Quadrant

For an acute angle θ, angles in other quadrants can be expressed as follows:

Quadrant II (90° - 180°)
sin(180° - θ) = sin θ
cos(180° - θ) = -cos θ
tan(180° - θ) = -tan θ
Sin +
Quadrant I (0° - 90°)
All ratios positive
sin θ, cos θ, tan θ
All +
Quadrant III (180° - 270°)
sin(180° + θ) = -sin θ
cos(180° + θ) = -cos θ
tan(180° + θ) = tan θ
Tan +
Quadrant IV (270° - 360°)
sin(360° - θ) = -sin θ
cos(360° - θ) = cos θ
tan(360° - θ) = -tan θ
Cos +
💡 Memory Aid - CAST: Starting from Q4 and going counter-clockwise: Cos, All, Sin, Tan tells you which ratios are positive in each quadrant.

3. Worked Example 1: Simplifying with Reduction Formulae

Simplify: cos 290° cos 320° + sin 110° sin 140°

Step 1: Express each angle as an acute angle

  • 290° = 360° - 70° (Quadrant IV) → cos 290° = cos 70°
  • 320° = 360° - 40° (Quadrant IV) → cos 320° = cos 40°
  • 110° = 180° - 70° (Quadrant II) → sin 110° = sin 70°
  • 140° = 180° - 40° (Quadrant II) → sin 140° = sin 40°

Step 2: Substitute the reduced angles

= cos 70° cos 40° + sin 70° sin 40°

Step 3: Recognize the compound angle pattern

= cos(70° - 40°) = cos 30°

Step 4: Evaluate the special angle

= √3/2

4. Worked Example 2: Proving Sum-to-Product Identities

Prove: cos 5A + cos A = 2cos 3A cos 2A

Strategy: Express 5A and A in terms of sums/differences that give the same pair of angles

• 5A = 3A + 2A

• A = 3A - 2A

1
LHS = cos 5A + cos A
Write the left hand side
2
= cos(3A + 2A) + cos(3A - 2A)
Rewrite 5A and A
3
= (cos 3A cos 2A - sin 3A sin 2A) + (cos 3A cos 2A + sin 3A sin 2A)
Apply compound angle identities
4
= cos 3A cos 2A + cos 3A cos 2A
The sin 3A sin 2A terms cancel
5
= 2 cos 3A cos 2A = RHS ✓
Combine like terms

🔑 Key Insight for Sum-to-Product

To prove identities like cos 5A + cos A = 2 cos 3A cos 2A:

  • Find two numbers that ADD to give one coefficient (5A) and SUBTRACT to give the other (A)
  • Here: 3A + 2A = 5A and 3A - 2A = A
  • Then use compound angle identities and watch terms cancel!
