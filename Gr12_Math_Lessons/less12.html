Arithmetic & Geometric Means

Series and Sequences

🎯

Learning Objectives

  • Define arithmetic and geometric means
  • Recall knowledge learnt to calculate Arithmetic Mean(s)
  • Determine geometric mean(s) between two numbers
  • Insert multiple geometric means between given terms
📋

Lesson Overview

In this lesson, we explore the concepts of arithmetic and geometric means. You will learn how to calculate means between two numbers and insert multiple means to form arithmetic or geometric sequences. Understanding these concepts is essential for working with series and sequences in Grade 12 Mathematics.

📚

Key Concepts & Principles

Arithmetic Mean (Review)

Arithmetic mean(s) are the term(s) inserted between two given numbers to form an arithmetic sequence.

Arithmetic Mean Formula

Arithmetic Mean = (a + c) / 2

Example: Arithmetic Mean

Find the Arithmetic Mean of 8 and 20:

1 Arithmetic Mean = (8 + 20) / 2
2 = 28 / 2 = 14
3 Sequence formed: 8; 14; 20 ✓

Geometric Mean

Important Note

If a, b, and c form a Geometric Sequence, then:

  • r = b/a = c/b (common ratio)
  • b² = a × c
  • b = ±√(a × c)

Geometric Mean Formula

Geometric Mean = ±√(a × c)

The Geometric Mean is also called the Mean Proportional.

Property Arithmetic Mean Geometric Mean
Formula (a + c) / 2 ±√(a × c)
Sequence Type Arithmetic Geometric
Common Operation Addition (common difference) Multiplication (common ratio)
Result One value Two values (±)
✏️

Worked Examples

Example 1: Find the Geometric Mean of 2 and 8

1 Using formula: Geometric Mean = ±√(a × c)
2 = ±√(2 × 8)
3 = ±√16 = ±4
4 Two geometric sequences: 2; 4; 8 and 2; -4; 8

Example 2: Insert 2 Geometric Means between 7 and -56

1 Sequence structure: 7; _; _; -56 (4 terms: T₁ = a = 7, T₄ = -56)
2 Using T₄ = a × r³: (7) × r³ = -56
3 r³ = -56/7 = -8
4 r = ∛(-8) = -2
5 T₂ = a × r = 7 × (-2) = -14
6 T₃ = a × r² = 7 × (-2)² = 7 × 4 = 28
7 Complete sequence: 7; -14; 28; -56 ✓
