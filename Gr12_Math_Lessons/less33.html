🔄 Revision: Key Identities

Double Angle Identities

Sine Double Angle

sin 2A = 2 sin A cos A

Cosine Double Angle (Form 1)

cos 2A = cos²A − sin²A

Cosine Double Angle (Form 2)

cos 2A = 2cos²A − 1

Cosine Double Angle (Form 3)

cos 2A = 1 − 2sin²A

Pythagorean Identity

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1

This can be rearranged to: sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ or cos²θ = 1 − sin²θ

📝 Problem-Solving Method

1 Identify the quadrant using the given restrictions (e.g., 0° < θ < 90° means Quadrant 1)
2 Draw a right triangle in the correct quadrant with the known sides
3 Use Pythagoras' Theorem to find the missing side: x² + y² = r²
4 Determine signs of x and y based on the quadrant
5 Apply the appropriate identity to calculate the required value

✏️ Worked Example 1

📝 Example 1: First Quadrant Problem
Given that cos θ = 35, where 0° < θ < 90°, find without using a calculator:

a) cos 2θ     b) sin 2θ
1 Identify the quadrant: Since 0° < θ < 90°, θ is in the first quadrant where all trig ratios are positive.
2 Draw the triangle:

From cos θ = xr = 35, we have x = 3 and r = 5

3 Use Pythagoras' Theorem to find y:
x² + y² = r²
y² = r² − x²
y² = 5² − 3² = 25 − 9 = 16
y = 4 (positive in Q1)
4 Calculate sin θ:
sin θ = yr = 45
5 Calculate cos 2θ:
cos 2θ = 2cos²θ − 1

= 2(35)² − 1

= 2 × 925 − 1

= 1825 − 2525

= −725
6 Calculate sin 2θ:
sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ

= 2 × 45 × 35

= 2425
Answers: a) cos 2θ = −725     b) sin 2θ = 2425

✏️ Worked Example 2

📝 Example 2: Third Quadrant Problem
If tan θ = 34 and 90° < θ < 360°, evaluate:

a) sin 2θ     b) cos 2θ
1 Identify the quadrant:

tan θ is positive in Q1 and Q3. The restriction 90° < θ < 360° eliminates Q1.

Therefore, θ is in the third quadrant.

In Q3: x < 0, y < 0, so x = −4 and y = −3

2 Draw the triangle in Q3:
3 Find r using Pythagoras:
r² = x² + y²
r² = (−4)² + (−3)² = 16 + 9 = 25
r = 5
4 Find sin θ and cos θ:
sin θ = yr = −35 = −35

cos θ = xr = −45 = −45
5 Calculate sin 2θ:
sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ

= 2 × (−35) × (−45)

= 2 × 1225

= 2425
6 Calculate cos 2θ:
cos 2θ = cos²θ − sin²θ

= (−45)² − (−35)²

= 1625 − 925

= 725
Answers: a) sin 2θ = 2425     b) cos 2θ = 725

✏️ Worked Example 3

📝 Example 3: Finding Single Angle from Double Angle
Given that cos 2x = 79, find the value of:

a) sin x     b) cos x
1 Choose the appropriate identity:

To find sin x, use: cos 2x = 1 − 2sin²x

To find cos x, use: cos 2x = 2cos²x − 1

2 Find sin x:
cos 2x = 1 − 2sin²x

79 = 1 − 2sin²x

2sin²x = 1 − 79 = 29

sin²x = 19

sin x = ±13
3 Find cos x:
cos 2x = 2cos²x − 1

79 = 2cos²x − 1

2cos²x = 79 + 1 = 169

cos²x = 89

cos x = ±2√23
⚠️ Note: Both answers have ± because without knowing which quadrant x is in, we cannot determine the sign. If given a restriction, we would choose the appropriate sign.
Answers: a) sin x = ±13     b) cos x = ±2√23
