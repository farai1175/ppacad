Geometric Sequences and Series

Lesson 4: Applying General Term & Sum Formulas to Complex Problems

📐 Key Formulas Review

In this lesson, we combine our knowledge of the general term formula with the sum formulas to solve more complex problems.

Tn = a · rn−1
General Term Formula
Sn = a(rn − 1)/r − 1
Sum Formula (when r ≠ 1)
💡 Remember:
  • Tn = the nth term
  • Sn = sum of the first n terms
  • a = first term
  • r = common ratio
  • n = number of terms
⚠️ Important: When solving r² = k (even power), you get two solutions: r = +√k OR r = −√k. Always check both possibilities!

✏️ Worked Examples

Example 1 Finding the sum when given two terms (odd exponent)

Problem: If the second term of a Geometric Series is 9 and the fifth term is 243, find the sum of 20 terms.

Solution:

1

Set up equations using Tn = a · rn−1:

T2 = a · r1 = 9 ... (1)
T5 = a · r4 = 243 ... (2)
2

Divide equation (2) by equation (1):

a · r4/a · r = 243/9
r3 = 27
r = 3

Note: Odd exponent gives only one solution

3

Substitute r = 3 into equation (1):

a · (3) = 9
a = 3
4

Calculate S20 using the sum formula:

Sn = a(rn − 1)/r − 1
S20 = 3(320 − 1)/3 − 1
S20 = 3(3,486,784,401 − 1)/2
S20 = 5,230,176,600

✓ The sum of 20 terms is 5,230,176,600

Example 2 Finding the sum when given two terms (even exponent - TWO solutions)

Problem: If the second term of a Geometric Series is 35 and the fourth term is 1,715, determine the sum of the first 8 terms of the series.

Solution:

1

Set up equations:

T2 = a · r = 35 ... (1)
T4 = a · r3 = 1715 ... (2)
2

Divide equation (2) by equation (1):

r2 = 1715/35 = 49
⚠️ Even exponent → TWO answers!
r = 7 OR r = −7
3

Find 'a' for each value of r:

If r = 7:

35 = a · (7)
a = 5

If r = −7:

35 = a · (−7)
a = −5
4

Calculate S8 for BOTH series:

Series 1: a = 5, r = 7

S8 = 5(78 − 1)/7 − 1
S8 = 5(5,764,801 − 1)/6
S8 = 4,804,000

Series 2: a = −5, r = −7

S8 = −5((−7)8 − 1)/−7 − 1
S8 = −5(5,764,801 − 1)/−8
S8 = 3,603,000

✓ Two possible answers: S8 = 4,804,000 OR S8 = 3,603,000
