🧮 Geometric Series Formulas

▼

Two Equivalent Formulas

The formula for the sum of a geometric series has two equivalent forms:

Formula 1 (when r > 1)

Sₙ = a(rⁿ - 1)/(r - 1)
Use when common ratio > 1

Formula 2 (when r < 1)

Sₙ = a(1 - rⁿ)/(1 - r)
Use when common ratio < 1
Both formulas give the same answer!
Choose based on which makes calculation easier
Where:
  • Sₙ = Sum of first n terms
  • a = First term
  • r = Common ratio (T₂/T₁)
  • n = Number of terms

📝 Important Notes:

• These formulas only work when r ≠ 1

• When r = 1, the series is constant: Sₙ = na

• Always check if |r| < 1 for infinite series convergence

• Remember to use brackets in your calculator!

1️⃣ Example 1: Basic Geometric Series

▼
Problem: Evaluate: 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + ... to 10 terms
  • Step 1: Identify parameters
    First term: a = 1
    Common ratio: r = 2/1 = 2
    Number of terms: n = 10
  • Step 2: Choose formula
    Since r = 2 > 1, use: Sₙ = a(rⁿ - 1)/(r - 1)
  • Step 3: Substitute values
    S₁₀ = 1 × (2¹⁰ - 1)/(2 - 1)
    S₁₀ = (2¹⁰ - 1)/1
    S₁₀ = 1024 - 1
  • Step 4: Calculate (with brackets!)
    On calculator: (2^10 - 1) = 1024 - 1 = 1023
    Remember: Always use brackets: (2^10 - 1) not 2^10 - 1

    • Answer: S₁₀ = 1023

    2️⃣ Example 2: Finding n and Sₙ

    ▼
    Problem: Given the series: 3 - 9 + 27 - 81 + ... -6561
    (a) Find n (number of terms)
    (b) Find Sₙ (sum of the series)
  • Part (a): Find n
    Step 1: Identify parameters
    First term: a = 3
    Common ratio: r = -9/3 = -3
    Last term: Tₙ = -6561
  • Step 2: Use general term formula
    Tₙ = arⁿ⁻¹
    -6561 = 3 × (-3)ⁿ⁻¹
    -6561/3 = (-3)ⁿ⁻¹
    -2187 = (-3)ⁿ⁻¹
  • Step 3: Express as powers of -3
    (-3)⁷ = -2187
    So: (-3)⁷ = (-3)ⁿ⁻¹
    7 = n - 1
    n = 8
  • Part (b): Find Sₙ
    Step 4: Use sum formula
    Since r = -3, use: Sₙ = a(rⁿ - 1)/(r - 1)
    S₈ = 3[(-3)⁸ - 1]/(-3 - 1)
    S₈ = 3(6561 - 1)/(-4)
    S₈ = 3 × 6560 / -4
    S₈ = 19680 / -4 = -4920

    • Answer: (a) n = 8, (b) S₈ = -4920

    📝 Important Point:

    Cannot use sum formula to find n! When only given last term, use the general term formula Tₙ = arⁿ⁻¹ to find n first, then use sum formula.

    Σ Sigma Notation

    ▼

    Sigma Notation: Σ (Summation Symbol)

    Σ (Sigma) means "the sum of"
    20
    Σ 3·2ᵏ⁻¹
    k=1
    Summation Symbol
    Σ
    Upper Limit
    20
    General Term
    3·2ᵏ⁻¹
    Index Variable
    k
    Lower Limit
    1

    How to Read Sigma Notation:

    Step 1: Start with k = lower limit (below Σ)

    Step 2: Substitute k into the general term formula

    Step 3: Increase k by 1 each time

    Step 4: Stop when k = upper limit (above Σ)

    Step 5: Add all the terms together

    Number of terms = Upper limit - Lower limit + 1 Always calculate this to know how many terms you're summing

    3️⃣ Example 3: Sigma Notation Calculation

    ▼
    20
    Σ 3·2ᵏ⁻¹
    k=1
    Manual Solution:
  • Step 1: Find T₁, T₂, T₃
    T₁ = 3·2¹⁻¹ = 3·2⁰ = 3
    T₂ = 3·2²⁻¹ = 3·2¹ = 6
    T₃ = 3·2³⁻¹ = 3·2² = 12
  • Step 2: Identify series type
    Series: 3 + 6 + 12 + ... to 20 terms (geometric)
    a = 3, r = 6/3 = 2, n = 20
  • Step 3: Use geometric sum formula
    Sₙ = a(rⁿ - 1)/(r - 1)
    S₂₀ = 3(2²⁰ - 1)/(2 - 1)
    S₂₀ = 3(1,048,576 - 1)/1
    S₂₀ = 3 × 1,048,575
  • Step 4: Calculate
    S₂₀ = 3,145,725

    • Answer: S₂₀ = 3,145,725

    📝 Different Sigma Notations:

    20
    Σ 3·2ᵏ⁻¹ = Sum of 21 terms (k from 0 to 20)
    k=0

    20
    Σ 3·2ᵏ⁻¹ = Sum of 18 terms (k from 3 to 20)
    k=3

    Formula: Number of terms = Top - Bottom + 1

    4️⃣ Example 4: Find m in Sigma Notation

    ▼
    m
    Σ 3·(-2)ⁿ⁻³ = 231
    n=1
    Problem: Determine m in the equation above
  • Step 1: Find first few terms
    T₁ = 3·(-2)¹⁻³ = 3·(-2)⁻² = 3 × ¼ = 3/4
    T₂ = 3·(-2)²⁻³ = 3·(-2)⁻¹ = 3 × (-½) = -3/2
    T₃ = 3·(-2)³⁻³ = 3·(-2)⁰ = 3 × 1 = 3
  • Step 2: Identify series
    Series: ¾ - ³⁄₂ + 3 - ... (geometric)
    a = ¾, r = (-³⁄₂) ÷ ¾ = -2
  • Step 3: Use sum formula
    Sₘ = a(rᵐ - 1)/(r - 1)
    231 = ¾[(-2)ᵐ - 1]/(-2 - 1)
    231 = ¾[(-2)ᵐ - 1]/(-3)
    231 × (-3) = ¾[(-2)ᵐ - 1]
    -693 = ¾[(-2)ᵐ - 1]
  • Step 4: Solve for m
    -693 × 4/3 = (-2)ᵐ - 1
    -924 = (-2)ᵐ - 1
    -923 = (-2)ᵐ
    This gives a negative number equal to (-2)ᵐ
    Since (-2)ᵐ alternates signs, check calculation...

    Actually, let's recalculate properly:
    231 = ¾[(-2)ᵐ - 1]/(-3)
    231 = -¼[(-2)ᵐ - 1]
    -924 = (-2)ᵐ - 1
    -923 = (-2)ᵐ (Not possible for integer m)

    Let's use the correct terms from the lesson:
    T₁ = 3·(-2)¹⁻³ = 3·(-2)⁻² = 3/4
    But wait, in the lesson example they got T₁ = -4
    Let's check: 3·(-2)⁻² = 3 × ¼ = 3/4 ✓
    There might be a different interpretation...

    • Note: The lesson example shows a different calculation. The key is to:

    1. Find the actual terms by substitution
    2. Identify a and r from those terms
    3. Set up the sum equation
    4. Solve for m using exponent rules

    ✓ Comprehensive Quiz (12 Questions)

    ▼

    Test your understanding of geometric series and sigma notation with these 12 questions.

    Question 1: What is the formula for Sₙ when r > 1?

    A) Sₙ = a(rⁿ - 1)/(r - 1)
    B) Sₙ = a(1 - rⁿ)/(1 - r)
    C) Sₙ = n/2[2a + (n-1)d]
    D) Sₙ = a + (n-1)d
    Explanation: When r > 1, use Sₙ = a(rⁿ - 1)/(r - 1). When r < 1, use Sₙ = a(1 - rⁿ)/(1 - r).

    Question 2: For 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + ... to 10 terms, what is S₁₀?

    A) 1020
    B) 1021
    C) 1022
    D) 1023
    Explanation: a=1, r=2, n=10. S₁₀ = 1(2¹⁰ - 1)/(2-1) = 1024 - 1 = 1023.

    Question 3: In sigma notation Σ, what does the number on top represent?

    A) First term
    B) Upper limit of summation
    C) Common ratio
    D) Number of terms minus one
    Explanation: The number on top of Σ is the upper limit - the last value of the index variable.

    Question 4: For 3 - 9 + 27 - 81 + ... -6561, what is n?

    A) 6
    B) 7
    C) 8
    D) 9
    Explanation: a=3, r=-3, Tₙ=-6561. Use Tₙ=arⁿ⁻¹: -6561=3(-3)ⁿ⁻¹ → (-3)⁷=(-3)ⁿ⁻¹ → 7=n-1 → n=8.

    Question 5: What does Σ (sigma) mean in mathematics?

    A) Sum of
    B) Product of
    C) Difference of
    D) Ratio of
    Explanation: Σ is the Greek letter sigma and represents summation or "the sum of".

    Question 6: For Σ 3·2ᵏ⁻¹ from k=1 to 20, how many terms are summed?

    A) 19
    B) 20
    C) 21
    D) 18
    Explanation: Number of terms = Upper limit - Lower limit + 1 = 20 - 1 + 1 = 20.

    Question 7: What is the first term for Σ 3·2ᵏ⁻¹ from k=1?

    A) 3
    B) 6
    C) 12
    D) 24
    Explanation: T₁ = 3·2¹⁻¹ = 3·2⁰ = 3×1 = 3.

    Question 8: For Σ 3·2ᵏ⁻¹ from k=1 to 20, what is S₂₀?

    A) 1,048,575
    B) 2,097,150
    C) 3,145,725
    D) 6,291,450
    Explanation: a=3, r=2, n=20. S₂₀ = 3(2²⁰ - 1)/(2-1) = 3(1,048,576 - 1) = 3×1,048,575 = 3,145,725.

    Question 9: What formula should you use to find n when given the last term?

    A) Sₙ formula
    B) Tₙ = arⁿ⁻¹
    C) Both formulas
    D) Neither formula
    Explanation: To find n when given the last term, use the general term formula Tₙ = arⁿ⁻¹.

    Question 10: For Σ 3·2ᵏ⁻¹ from k=0 to 20, how many terms are summed?

    A) 19
    B) 20
    C) 21
    D) 22
    Explanation: Number of terms = 20 - 0 + 1 = 21.

    Question 11: Why must you use brackets when calculating (2¹⁰ - 1) on a calculator?

    A) To make it look neat
    B) To ensure correct order of operations
    C) Calculators require brackets
    D) It's optional
    Explanation: Without brackets, 2^10 - 1 might be interpreted as 2^(10 - 1) = 2⁹ = 512 instead of (2¹⁰) - 1 = 1023.

    Question 12: What happens when r = 1 in a geometric series?

    A) Sₙ = na (constant series)
    B) Use Sₙ = a(rⁿ - 1)/(r - 1)
    C) Series diverges to infinity
    D) Formula doesn't change
    Explanation: When r = 1, all terms are equal to a, so Sₙ = a + a + ... + a (n times) = na.
